Warnings

  • Bleeding-edge / custom firmware. Expect unfinished work, crashes, and data loss. Back up your projects first.
  • Modified firmware can brick or soft-brick the unit and voids normal support expectations. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Elektron.
  • No Octatrack firmware is hosted here. You provide your own official OCTATRACK_OS1.40C.syx. The patched image is built entirely in the browser.
  • Do not redistribute the generated .bin / .syx — they contain Elektron’s OS. Share this page instead.
  • Flash only via CompactFlash: copy 1.40MIDISC5.bin to the card root → OS UPGRADE. Keep stock 1.40C for recovery (MIDI or CF).
